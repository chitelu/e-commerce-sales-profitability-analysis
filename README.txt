# E-Commerce Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses an e-commerce sales dataset to evaluate business performance, identify profitability trends, assess product and regional performance, and provide data-driven recommendations to management.

The analysis was conducted using Microsoft Excel, with PivotTables, calculated metrics, and charts to transform raw transaction data into meaningful business insights.

## Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this project were to:

- Evaluate overall sales and profitability.
- Analyse sales and profit trends over time.
- Compare performance across product categories and geographical regions.
- Identify the most profitable and least profitable products.
- Identify areas of weak profitability and recommend improvements.
- Develop an dashboard for business performance monitoring.

## Tools and Techniques

**Tool:** Microsoft Excel

**Techniques used:**
- Data cleaning and validation
- Excel formulas and calculated metrics
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Sales and profitability analysis
- Trend analysis
- Interactive dashboard development

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Regional Performance

The West region recorded the highest total profit of **$110,798.82**, making it the strongest regional contributor to profitability.

### 2. Category Performance

Technology was the most profitable product category, generating **$146,543.38** in profit.

Furniture presented a profitability concern, generating $754,747.76 in sales but only $19,730.00 in profit, representing a profit margin of approximately 7.54%.

### 3. Product Performance

The Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier was the most profitable product, generating **$25,199.93** in profit.

The Cubify CubeX 3D Printer Double Head Print recorded the largest product-level loss of **$8,879.97**.

### 4. Overall Profitability

The business generated $292,296.81 in profit from $2,326,534.35 in sales, resulting in an overall profit margin of **12.56%**.

## Recommendations to Management

Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:

1. **Improve Furniture profitability:** Review pricing strategies, discount policies and procurement costs to address the category's low profit margin.
2. **Strengthen regional performance:** Investigate the operational and commercial practices contributing to the West's strong performance and assess opportunities to improve weaker regions.
3. **Prioritise profitable products:** Maintain availability of high-performing products and assess opportunities to increase their contribution to overall profit.
4. **Review loss-making products:** Investigate products recording significant losses and consider repricing, cost reduction or discontinuation where appropriate.
5. **Monitor profitability continuously:** Track sales, profit and profit margins together to ensure revenue growth translates into sustainable profitability.
